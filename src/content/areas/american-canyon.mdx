## Pest control in American Canyon

American Canyon is one of the newer cities in Napa County — most of its housing was built between the 1990s and 2010s, with ongoing development continuing today. The newer construction means fewer of the structural gaps that plague Vallejo's older housing stock, but American Canyon has its own pest profile driven by its suburban-edge location between Vallejo and Napa.

The city is surrounded by open grassland and light-use agricultural land. That edge habitat supports large gopher and ground squirrel populations, which in turn attract rattlesnakes, hawks, and coyotes. Argentine ants are ubiquitous — Bay Area soil, mild climate, year-round pressure.

## Common pest problems in American Canyon

- **Argentine ants** — the primary household pest call, especially in spring. American Canyon's newer home construction has fewer structural gaps, but Argentine ants enter through door thresholds, plumbing penetrations, and weep holes regardless of home age.
- **Gophers** — common in American Canyon's adjacent open land and newer landscaped yards. We treat active burrow systems and provide exclusion for raised beds and turf areas.
- **Spiders** — black widows are present throughout the area and common in garages, under deck boards, and in outdoor storage. Not an infestation but a legitimate safety concern for homes with children.
- **Rodents** — mice and Norway rats enter from the open-land buffer, especially in fall when temperatures drop and outdoor food sources thin out.

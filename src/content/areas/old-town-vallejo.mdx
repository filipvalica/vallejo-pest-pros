## Pest control in Old Town Vallejo

Old Town Vallejo is one of the oldest settled parts of the city, Victorian-era homes, 1910s–1930s craftsman bungalows, and turn-of-the-century commercial buildings along Georgia Street. That history is what makes it one of Vallejo's higher-pressure pest neighborhoods.

Structures built before 1940 were framed to different tolerances than modern construction. Foundation sills shrink over decades. Crawl space vents rust and crumble. Gaps around original plumbing are wide enough for a rat to walk through. A home that was perfectly sealed in 1925 may have a dozen rodent entry points today.

The waterfront adds to the pressure. Old Town sits directly on the Mare Island Strait. Norway rats are common along the waterfront and push inland through storm drains and landscaping. When food or nesting sites get scarce near the water, residential neighborhoods like Old Town are the next stop.

## Common pest problems in Old Town

- **Norwegian and roof rats**: the most common call we get in Old Town. Older homes have extensive crawl space access and unprotected eave gaps that roof rats exploit.
- **Argentine ants**: omnipresent in the Bay Area; Old Town's damp soil conditions near the waterfront keep colonies well-watered and aggressive.
- **Cockroaches**: specifically Oriental roaches in older multi-family buildings near downtown, where they enter through aging sewer lines and floor drains.
- **Termites**: subterranean termites in wood crawl space members; older sill plates with direct soil contact are the primary target.

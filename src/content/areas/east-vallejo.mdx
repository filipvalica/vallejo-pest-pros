## Pest control in East Vallejo

East Vallejo's housing stock is predominantly mid-century ranch-style homes built between the late 1950s and mid-1970s. These homes have characteristics that make pest exclusion a recurring challenge: slab foundations with wide expansion joint gaps, wood siding that has dried and split over decades, and crawl space vents that were undersized by current standards when new.

The neighborhood's proximity to industrial corridors along Sonoma Boulevard also matters. Businesses and warehouses attract rodent populations that spill into adjacent residential streets as their outdoor food sources fluctuate.

## Common pest problems in East Vallejo

- **Argentine ants**: the dominant pest call in East Vallejo. The neighborhood's larger lots mean more soil surface for colonies to establish. After winter rains, foraging trails move indoors through foundation cracks and weep holes.
- **Rodents**: Norway rats are common along the industrial corridor; roof rats exploit older eave lines and utility penetrations in the 1960s–70s homes.
- **Fleas**: larger lots with outdoor pets see higher flea pressure, especially in fall when wildlife traffic through yards increases.
- **Earwigs and pill bugs**: moisture-loving pests that build up in ground-contact wood and damp crawl space areas common in older East Vallejo construction.

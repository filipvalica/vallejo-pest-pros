## Pest control in Benicia

Benicia sits at the narrows of the Carquinez Strait, 10–15 minutes from downtown Vallejo via I-680. Like Vallejo, it has a significant stock of older homes — the historic First Street district has Victorian and Craftsman properties from the 1880s–1920s — and a waterfront corridor that supports persistent rodent populations.

The city is small enough that it's frequently underserved by the larger regional pest control chains. Most Benicia residents call Vallejo-based operators, and we're one of the closer options.

## Common pest problems in Benicia

- **Argentine ants** — Bay Area-wide; Benicia's hillside homes see trails move indoors when dry-season heat concentrates colonies near water sources inside the structure.
- **Roof rats** — common in hillside neighborhoods where large trees provide access routes to rooflines.
- **Termites** — subterranean termites in the historic downtown district; older sill plates and wood porch framing are the typical entry point.
- **Yellow jackets and wasps** — hillside properties with landscaped gardens see higher nest-building pressure in spring.

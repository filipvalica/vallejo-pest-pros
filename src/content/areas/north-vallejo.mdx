## Pest control in North Vallejo

North Vallejo covers the neighborhoods north of Interstate 80 toward the Napa River flood plain, including the areas near Six Flags Discovery Kingdom. Most of the housing here was built in the 1950s and 1960s, post-WWII suburban development that brought families to what was then the edge of the city.

That era of construction sits at a similar structural age to East Vallejo: old enough to have foundation gaps, rusted crawl space vents, and dried exterior caulk, but not old enough to have been through a major rehabilitation cycle. Argentine ants know every one of those gaps.

The neighborhood's proximity to the Napa River and the lower-lying areas near the marina also creates elevated rodent pressure. Norway rats are common along the riparian corridor and push inland through storm drain systems when populations build.

## North Vallejo pest profile

- **Argentine ants**: spring surge is the primary call. Post-rain ant trails moving through foundation weep holes and door thresholds are the typical complaint.
- **Rodents**: Norway rats near the river corridor; roof rats in older homes with tree canopy over rooflines.
- **Fleas**: families with dogs and yard access see flea pressure ramp up in late summer and fall as wildlife activity peaks.
- **Wasps**: North Vallejo's open lots and established trees see more yellow jacket and paper wasp nesting activity in spring than denser urban neighborhoods.

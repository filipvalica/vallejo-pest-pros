## Year-round pest pressure in Vallejo

Vallejo's Bay Area climate means pest pressure doesn't take a winter break. There's no killing frost to reset populations. Ants, spiders, earwigs, and fleas are active in every month of the year, with surges in spring (ants post-rain) and fall (everything looking for warmth).

The result is that one-time treatments for common household pests tend to wear off and leave you calling again in three months. A quarterly maintenance program maintains a treated perimeter continuously, which catches the next wave before it establishes inside.

## What the service covers

**Initial service:**
- Interior and exterior inspection to identify active pests, entry points, and harborage sites
- Interior treatment where activity is found (baseboards, under sinks, around appliances)
- Full exterior treatment: foundation band, window and door frames, eaves, and landscape border
- Entry-point sealing where practical (door sweeps, weep holes, gaps around pipes)

**Quarterly maintenance:**
- Exterior perimeter refresh
- Interior spot-treatment if any new activity reported since last visit
- Inspection for signs of rodent or termite activity (flagged and quoted separately if found)

## Pests we handle

- **Ants** (Argentine ants, pavement ants, odorous house ants)
- **Spiders** (including black widows, which are present throughout Solano County)
- **Fleas**: common in Vallejo homes with pets, especially with outdoor access
- **Earwigs and pill bugs**: moisture-loving; common in crawl spaces and damp perimeter beds
- **Silverfish**: older homes with cellulose materials (books, cardboard, wallpaper)
- **Wasps and yellow jackets**: seasonal (spring nest-building)

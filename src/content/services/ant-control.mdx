## The ant problem in Vallejo

Argentine ants thrive in Vallejo's mild, foggy Bay Area climate. Unlike inland CA where summer heat knocks populations back, Vallejo's year-round moderate temperatures let colonies grow without interruption. The result: ant pressure that doesn't have an off-season.

Most calls come March through June, when winter rains end and foraging ramps up. But waterfront neighborhoods near Mare Island and San Pablo Bay see activity year-round because of consistent soil moisture in crawl spaces and near foundations.

## What the job involves

We start with a perimeter inspection — tracking entry points along the foundation sill, around utility penetrations, and under door sweeps. Argentine ants almost always have multiple entry points; sealing one without treating the others just redirects the trail.

Treatment includes:
- Gel bait placed at active trail sites (carried back to the colony)
- Exterior perimeter spray along the foundation, window frames, and eaves
- Entry-point sealing for gaps we can close on the spot

Interior spray is used only when necessary. Gel bait is more effective for Argentine ants and avoids unnecessary chemical exposure indoors.

## Why hire a pro

Argentine ants aren't a one-spray problem. Their colonies are decentralized — a single super-colony can span multiple yards and hundreds of thousands of workers. Over-the-counter sprays kill the foragers you see but scatter the colony, which often makes the problem worse for 1–3 days before it gets better.

A licensed applicator uses bait formulations and placements that target the colony structure, not just the trail.

## Cockroaches in Vallejo's older housing stock

German and Oriental cockroaches thrive in the conditions Vallejo's older housing provides: tight cabinet gaps, aging plumbing, crawl spaces with soil contact, and shared walls in apartment buildings. Old Town and East Vallejo have the highest density of 1960s–70s multi-family units where building-to-building spread is a persistent problem.

Oriental roaches specifically exploit Vallejo's waterfront proximity — they enter through floor drains, gaps around pipe penetrations, and damaged crawl space vents that connect to the municipal sewer system.

## What the job involves

German cockroaches require gel bait treatment, not spray. Spray kills contact-exposed roaches but doesn't reach the colony hiding in wall voids, appliance motors, and cabinet hinges. Gel bait gets carried back to the harborage and taken by roaches that never touch the treated surface.

- **Inspection** — identify species, harborage sites, and infestation severity
- **Gel bait application** — placed in cracks, hinges, under appliances, behind outlets
- **Crack-and-crevice spray** — used on harborage zones when infestation is heavy
- **Drain treatment** — addresses Oriental roach entry points
- **Follow-up at 3–4 weeks** — gel bait depletes; a second visit addresses survivors and confirms elimination

## Why hire a pro

The most common reason over-the-counter roach treatments fail is product selection. Spray-and-pray kills the visible roaches and repels the rest, scattering the population deeper into the wall void. Licensed applicators use non-repellent gel baits that work with the roaches' behavior rather than against it.

For German roaches especially, a single surviving egg case (each holds 30–40 eggs) can restart an infestation.
